How to interpret this network

Nodes represent isolates assigned to the same inferred transmission cluster. Edges form a minimum spanning tree based on SNP distance for visualization and do not represent direct transmission or necessarily satisfy the configured temporal cutoff of 14 days.

Transmission-cluster membership may reflect a qualifying chain through intermediate isolates. Solid edges connect isolates whose direct collection-date difference is within the temporal cutoff. Dashed edges connect isolates whose direct collection-date difference exceeds the cutoff.

Review the corresponding transmission scatterplot for the temporal distribution and possible time-based chain of cases. Hover over nodes and edges for metadata and distance details.
