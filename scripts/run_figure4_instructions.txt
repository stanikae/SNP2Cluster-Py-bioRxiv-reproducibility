FIGURE 4 REPRODUCIBLE GENERATION
================================

Purpose
-------
Generate the final four-panel Figure 4 for the Ghana pneumococcal
Community analysis using SNP2Cluster-Py v1.1.2 outputs.

Final selected lineages
-----------------------
ST802:
    12 transmission-clustered isolates
    1 Community transmission cluster
    6 sampling locations: AME, DAN, GS, H1, NEW and UIC

ST5103:
    7 transmission-clustered isolates
    1 Community transmission cluster
    5 sampling locations: GS, IMA, KAK, NEW and PEDU

Final panel layout
------------------
A. ST802 temporal scatterplot  | B. ST802 Community network
C. ST5103 temporal scatterplot | D. ST5103 Community network

SNP2Cluster-Py v1.1.2

    a4535d47beecfde1d6dc66097e0b78580032afc6

14. Final manuscript caption

Figure 4. Community-level analysis of pneumococcal carriage isolates
across sampling locations. (A) Temporal and geographical distribution
of ST802 isolates across AME, DAN, GS, H1, NEW and UIC. (B) Genomic
relationships among 12 transmission-clustered ST802 isolates
represented as a single Community network spanning six sampling
locations. (C) Temporal and geographical distribution of ST5103
isolates across GS, IMA, KAK, NEW and PEDU. (D) Genomic relationships
among seven transmission-clustered ST5103 isolates represented as a
single Community network spanning five sampling locations. Community
clusters were identified using a 12-SNP threshold and a 45-day temporal
window. Node colours indicate Community-cluster assignment, node shapes
indicate sampling location, and edges show within-cluster
minimum-spanning-tree relationships labelled by SNP distance. Solid
edges connect isolates whose direct collection-date difference is
within 45 days, whereas dashed edges connect isolates whose direct date
difference exceeds 45 days. Network edges support visual interpretation
and do not imply direct or directional transmission.
